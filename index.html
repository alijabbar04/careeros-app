CareerOS

CareerOS is a personal job-search assistant built by Ali Jabbar for his own use. It runs on his own laptop and is not offered to anyone else.

It keeps track of the roles he applies for, reads the replies that arrive by email, and puts interview and assessment times in his calendar. Nothing is sent from his account unless he presses Send himself.

To do this, CareerOS connects to his Google account from the laptop it runs on. It reads and labels his job-application emails, saves drafts for him to review, sends only an email he has approved, and adds events to his calendar. What it stores stays on the laptop, with backups in his own OneDrive.
